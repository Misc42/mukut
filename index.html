Four systems on one chin-bar module.

Vision

Dual 180° rear cameras → a live HUD in the corner of your eye, with blind-spot motion alerts.

Audio

Noise-cancelled mic array + helmet speakers. Music and calls mix in; group voice ducks for calls.

Radios

Three radios, one mesh: pack voice at 1–2 km, spread voice at 3–5 km, SOS at 15–20 km. No SIM.

Power

Two hours internal, then any USB-PD power bank you already own via magnetic contacts.

Privacy Policy · effective 27 April 2026

There is no Mukut cloud.

TL;DR — read this if nothing else

  • We do not run a server. There is no Mukut cloud.
  • Your recordings live on the helmet's microSD and your phone — nowhere else.
  • Mesh voice is never recorded or routed through the internet.
  • No analytics, no ads, no data sales. If a future feature changes this, the app shows a plain-language diff first.

1. Who we are

Mukut is a clip-on safety module for ISI-certified motorcycle helmets, designed and built in India. The Mukut Companion app is the Android-side interface to the module. Operator and data controller: Misc42 Labs (sole proprietorship, India). Written to comply with India's DPDP Act 2023, Google Play Data Safety, and the spirit of GDPR data-minimisation.

2. What the app collects, stores, processes

Split by where data lives, because where matters more than what: ride history and settings stay on your phone; camera recordings stay on the helmet's microSD. A future opt-in lets you point offload at a server URL you own (NAS, Nextcloud, S3) — off by default.

3. What the app does NOT collect

Commitments, not aspirations: no location sold or shared, no audio recordings of mesh voice, no contact lists, no advertising identifiers, no analytics events leaving the device.

4. Permissions, and why

Bluetooth (pairing the module), Wi-Fi/network (joining the helmet's AP), notifications (SOS + ride state), location only as Android requires for BLE scanning. We do not request phone state, contacts, microphone, camera, or all-files access.

5. BLE pairing and link security

BLE Secure Connections with numeric-comparison or passkey. A per-pairing key authenticates the Wi-Fi join; the helmet's AP uses WPA2-PSK with a per-helmet password printed on the module and rotatable from the app.

6. Audio and video residency

Mesh voice between riders is encoded on the helmet and transmitted over the active radio — not recorded, not stored, never routed through the internet. Camera footage writes to the helmet's microSD only.

7. Retention

Phone-side ride history: kept until you delete it (long-press a ride → Delete, or Settings → Wipe all rides). Helmet-side recordings: circular buffer on the microSD; oldest footage is overwritten first.

8. Your rights

Under DPDP 2023 and applicable EU/UK rules: access, correction, erasure, and grievance redressal. Since your data lives on your own devices, most of these are actions you perform directly in the app.

9. Children

Not directed at users under 18 — motorcycles in India require a licence. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. Security posture, honestly

All phone-to-helmet traffic rides a local Wi-Fi link with WPA2-PSK plus an app-layer per-pairing key. A v1 prototype is not a hardened product; the honest-scope page says exactly what is and isn't certified.

11. International transfers

None — we do not collect your data on a server. If you use the future self-hosted upload URL, you are the controller of that destination.

12. Changes to this policy

Meaningful changes surface as a plain-language diff in the app that you must acknowledge. Cosmetic edits are recorded in version history.

13. Data deletion

Most deletion is a two-tap action on your own device: wipe rides in the app, format the microSD from Settings, unpair to forget keys. This satisfies the Google Play data-deletion disclosure.

14. Contact

General privacy: privacy@mukutsenses.com. Grievance officer (DPDP): reachable at privacy@mukutsenses.com.
